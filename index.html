Follow the build See the concept

THE IDEA

Desktop speakers that do not occupy your desktop

Traditional desktop speakers take up valuable room beside a monitor. ClampSound explores a slimmer approach: speaker enclosures that attach to the desk edge, with attention to safe clamping, vibration control, cable routing, and near-field sound quality.

PROTOTYPE ROADMAP

From concept to a working prototype

01 — Design

Define dimensions, clamp range, drivers, enclosure volume, and cable routing.

02 — 3D print

Create the first enclosure and integrated clamp mechanism.

03 — Test

Check desk fit, stability, vibration, usability, and sound quality.

04 — Improve

Share results publicly, collect feedback, and refine the design.
