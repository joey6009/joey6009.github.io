DESKTOP AUDIO CONCEPT

More sound.
Less desk.

A slim desktop speaker concept designed to mount beside a monitor, reclaim desk space, and bring near-field audio closer to the listener.

View the renderings Follow the build

THE CONCEPT

Speakers that work around your monitor

Desktop speakers normally occupy valuable space on either side of a display. This concept explores tall, slim speaker enclosures placed beside a monitor, with a mounting approach intended to reduce the footprint on the desk.

The current design includes multiple front-facing drivers, a compact side profile, and a separate small driver section. The next phase is to validate enclosure volume, driver selection, mounting safety, vibration control, and real acoustic performance with a physical prototype.
